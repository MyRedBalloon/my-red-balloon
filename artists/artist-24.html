Missy K Art

Missy K is a British contemporary artist whose vibrant, expressive work celebrates colour, emotion, resilience and the joy of being alive.

With a background in fine art, music and performance, she returned to painting in 2017, developing a distinctive style that combines acrylics with mixed media, texture, metallic finishes and unexpected details.

Often described as ‘dopamine art’, her work is bold, playful and full of personality.
