Adam Fenikowski

Adam Fenikowski (b. 1956, Kołczewo, Poland) is a London based artist whose international career spans Europe and Australia. He studied Building and Architecture at Szczecin Polytechnic.

Subsequently he relocated to Sydney, Australia, where from 1998 to 2006, he was represented by Marlene Antico Fine Art, one of Sydney’s leading galleries, holding 6 solo exhibitions during this period.
