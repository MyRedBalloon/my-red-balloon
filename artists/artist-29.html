Jacqueline Hughes

Jacqueline Hughes is a Somerset-based sustainable textile artist whose practice explores the relationship between personal experience, memory, resilience and the human spirit.

A mature graduate with a First-Class BA (Hons) working primarily with cyanotype on fabric, hand and machine embroidery, appliqué and fabric manipulation, Jacqueline transforms reclaimed and vintage materials into richly layered, narrativedriven artworks.
