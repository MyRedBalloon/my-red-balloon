Jonathan Payne

Jonathan Payne (JP) is a British contemporary spraypaint artist whose work combines the bold visual language of street art with the cultural resonance of pop art.

His original, handsprayed pieces bring energy, personality and a distinctive presence to a space, pairing immediate visual impact with narratives that reward closer attention.
