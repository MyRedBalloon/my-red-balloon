Carrieanne Vivianette

Carrieanne Vivianette produces performance work that is experimental with physical and vocal expression and the sequencing of text and choreography, delivering vignettes of spoken word, performative poetry, comical drama and improvised action.

She has a particular interest in the avant-garde and how artists express their personal philosophies, as seen in her recent performance project, ‘Manifestations of Female Innovation’.
