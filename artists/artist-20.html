Ros Burgin

Ros Burgin is a sculptor who combines globally traded materials and a variety of processes and industrial methods, discovering new ways to shape and inform sculpture.

She makes work that considers values and comments on sustainability, the marine environment and the part women play in shaping society. Her practice covers drawing, sculpture, installation and an engagement with a sense of time and place.
