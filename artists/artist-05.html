Nina Bumbalkova

Nina Bumbalkova is a Czech born London-based visual artist working across photography and moving image. Her practice is grounded in documentary observation and visual research, exploring themes of memory, identity, and place through long-term, process-driven work.

She is particularly interested in how personal and collective histories are shaped by landscape, cultural transition, and shared experience. Working between still and moving image, Bumbalkova often blurs the boundaries between cinematic and photographic forms.
