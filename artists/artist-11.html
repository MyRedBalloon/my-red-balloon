Olga Minasyan

Olga Minasyan is a contemporary painter based in Hertfordshire, UK. Her work explores the relationship between light, atmosphere, memory.

Through light, reflections and atmosphere, she seeks to capture the quiet stories and emotions hidden within everyday places.
