Evelyn J Marshall

Evelyn J Marshall is a contemporary painter and filmmaker from London, he seeks to create symbolic paintings and films that explore the absurdities of contemporary life.

Combining elements of theatre, satire, social observation and existential philosophy, his works depict individuals navigating systems of performance, identity and collective behaviour. Through densely layered narratives.
