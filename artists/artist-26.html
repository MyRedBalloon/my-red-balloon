Bex Willans

Bex Willans is a Kent based contemporary glass artist specializing in stained and kiln-formed glass.

Her practice serves as a critical exploration of contemporary social issues; including the prejudices surrounding aging, disability, and inter-racial relationships as well as the profound impact of humanity on the natural environment.
