Alaina Cherry

Alaina Cherry is an American artist based in London, UK who explores the intersections between black femininity, pleasure, and power.

She moved to London from Boston, Massachusetts in the fall of 2024 to pursue her MFA in fine art at Goldsmiths, University of London.
