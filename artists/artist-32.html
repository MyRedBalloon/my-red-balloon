Linda Chapman

Linda Chapman studied Photography at Westminster college after working as a photographic fine art assistant in a London studio. She went on to work freelance in theatres, model portfolios and music.

Eventually, she moved onto concentrating fully on her personal work, and has exhibited internationally, earning several awards, including the Art Gemini prize in 2025.
