Henryk Terpilowski

Henryk Terpiłowski is a London-based artist working across sculpture, assemblage and installation. He studied Three Dimensional Design at Wolverhampton Polytechnic and sculpture at the Academy of Fine Arts in Warsaw.

His background in window display, decorative painting and prop making informs his hands-on approach to materials and making. Henryk combines found, reclaimed and unconventional materials, allowing unexpected forms and meanings to emerge through the process.
