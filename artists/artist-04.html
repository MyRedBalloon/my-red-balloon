Sharon Samms

Sharon Flowers is a South London-based multidisciplinary artist whose creative practice encompasses textile art, photography, painting and stained glass.

With a background in Art and Design, Sharon explores identity, cultural heritage, human emotion and our relationship with the natural world. Her work draws inspiration from African and Caribbean artistic traditions, combining traditional craftsmanship with contemporary approaches to mixed-media art.
