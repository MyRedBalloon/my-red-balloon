Nicola J Reid

Nicola J Reid is a London based multidisciplinary artist, designer, fibre artist and cultural craft practitioner whose work explores identity, heritage, sustainability and the emotional connections between people, objects and the world around them.

Drawing on her Ghanaian and British heritage, Nicola brings together traditional craft techniques and contemporary artistic practices to create expressive paintings, sculptures, innovative fashion, textile artworks and largescale installations.
