Marcia Harding

Marcia Harding is a visual artist who creates work in a range of mediums, including photography. She began her artistic journey after a career in education. Her work focuses on her identity and memories as a Guyanese-British woman.

Her photograph taken in April 2020 while on a walk in Southern Cemetery during lockdown, won her the Rankin 2020 award, Nature category, and featured in Rankin’s book: ‘2020, A Year Like No Other’, and she also appeared on SkyArts.
