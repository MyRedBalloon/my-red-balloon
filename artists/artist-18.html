Julien Ipanga

Julien Ipanga (b.1997) is a mix media sculptor who combines in his work, history of life and death, translating them into a present day narrative. At his studio in Peckham, South London he’s developed his practice in sculpture.

He explores themes of the expansion of the human condition (as previously mentioned in both life and death throughout history) through figurative sculpture work, utilising different types of natural wood, resin, red clay and metals.
