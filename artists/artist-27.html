Nesha Mistry

Nesha (pronounced Nee-sha) Mistry is an Artist and Emotional Wellness Mentor, passionate about reconnecting people to joy, creativity and the benefits these bring to mental health and emotional wellbeing.

Drawing on her own lived experience, Nesha curates and hosts educational, research-informed art workshops for adults and children. Her workshops offer a break from everyday noise and overwhelm, creating welcoming spaces to slow down, explore self-expression and reconnect with curiosity and their inner child, all without pressure or perfection.

More than just art, her sessions encourage conversation, connection and emotional awareness, helping people discover practical ways to support their wellbeing through creativity.
