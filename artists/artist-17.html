Katarzyna Kowalska

I am a Polish-born, London based artist who returned to painting in May 2026 after a thirty-year break. My artistic practice explores memory, identity, motherhood, time and the experiences that shape my understanding of myself and the world around me.

Working primarily with acrylic and mixed media, I create figurative paintings inspired by personal photographs, memories and moments from everyday life.
