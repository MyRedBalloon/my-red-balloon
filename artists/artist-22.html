Anna Yakusheva

Anna Yakusheva (b. 1991) is a Ukrainian contemporary artist based in the UK. Her practice explores consciousness, identity and the ways we perceive and interpret reality.

Through painting, magazine clippings and symbolic imagery, she examines how our understanding of ourselves and the world is shaped, inviting viewers to question familiar assumptions and reconsider their own experience.
