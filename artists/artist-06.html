Jane Morgan

Jane Morgan is a British conceptual artist. She studied at London Guildhall University, achieving a First-Class BA Honours and a Master’s degree in Fine Art. Her emerging talent was recognised, when she was selected as a Bloomberg New Contemporary, showcasing her expansive, and captivating photographic images.

Her practice has since evolved, and has developed through both large and small-scale sculptural installations. Her methodology is consistent and distinctly expressive, characterised with rigour, precision, and editorial immediacy.
